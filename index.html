CheckMyJobJob Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

CheckMyJobJob is a work scheduling and reminder app. It allows users to save employers, pay rates, shifts, work locations, and alarm preferences.

Information you enter

Job names, hourly pay rates, workplace addresses or map links, shift schedules, and alarm settings are saved locally on your device. The current version does not create user accounts or upload this schedule information to servers operated by CheckMyJobJob.

Location search and maps

When you choose to search for or save a workplace location, the app may ask your device's geocoding service to find the address. That service may process the search query under its own privacy practices. The app does not request background or precise device-location permission. Opening directions uses the maps application you choose.

Alarms and device access

The app uses alarm, notification, vibration, and boot-completed capabilities to provide work reminders and restore alarms. It does not use those permissions to track your movements.

Sharing and schedule files

When you choose to share a schedule image, it is generated on your device and sent only to the app or recipient you select. Workplace locations are excluded from shared images unless you choose to include them. CSV import/export is marked Pro and is not available in this closed-test version.

Advertising, purchases, and analytics

This closed-test build does not activate advertising, paid purchases, or developer-operated analytics. These practices may change in future versions, in which case the policy will be updated.

Storage and backups

Data remains in the app's device storage until you edit/delete it or remove the app. Android device-backup features, if enabled, may back up app data through the operating system or backup provider. Temporary shared images are kept in the app's cache and may be cleared by Android or when app data is removed.

Security and deletion

We rely on Android's app storage isolation and the sharing permissions granted for user-initiated exports. You can remove individual saved job and shift information in the app. Clearing its storage or uninstalling the app removes local data; device backups may need to be deleted separately using device or backup-account controls.

Children

CheckMyJobJob is not designed specifically for children.

Privacy inquiries

Contact the developer at broughtvulture.apps@gmail.com. You can also use the developer contact link on the CheckMyJobJob Google Play listing.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change.
