CheckMyJobJob Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Overview

CheckMyJobJob is a work scheduling and reminder app. You can save jobs, hourly pay rates, work assignments, shifts, workplace locations, and work-alarm preferences. This policy explains how that information is handled and how optional advertising and Google Play purchases work.

Version availability: The v0.4.1 Google Play closed-testing build makes Earnings Projection and CSV Import/Export available without payment and does not show ads or offer a Pro purchase. In the separately developed v0.4.2 monetization build, these tools may require an optional Lifetime Pro purchase, and the free version may display an AdMob banner. Live ads are not yet enabled in the release configuration. This policy covers those features when they are enabled in an applicable build.

Information you enter

Job names, wages, work assignments, shift schedules and actual times, unpaid breaks, saved presets, workplace addresses or map links, and alarm preferences are stored locally on your device. CheckMyJobJob does not require an app account and does not send your saved job or shift records to servers operated by the developer.

Workplace location search and maps

If you search for a workplace, your device's geocoding service may process your query to return a location. Its handling of that query is subject to the service provider's privacy practices. If you open directions, the maps app you choose receives the destination you selected. CheckMyJobJob does not request background or precise device-location permission.

Alarms and device capabilities

The app uses alarm, notification, vibration, and boot-completed capabilities for work reminders and to restore scheduled alarms. These capabilities are not used by the developer to track your movement.

Schedule sharing, import, and export

Weekly schedule images are generated on your device and shared only when you select an app or recipient. Workplace addresses are omitted from shared images unless you choose to include them. CSV Import/Export allows you to save or load schedule files through Android's document picker. Once you export or share a file, its handling depends on the destination or recipient you choose.

Advertising and data handled by Google

Ad-supported builds of CheckMyJobJob may display a banner provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). When advertising is active, Google may automatically collect and process data such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising and app/device identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This data may be used for ad delivery, measurement, analytics related to advertising, and fraud prevention.

The app does not send your saved employer names, wages, shift schedule, or work assignments to AdMob as ad-targeting information. CheckMyJobJob does not run a separate developer-operated analytics service in the builds described here. For details of Google's processing and controls, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Development builds can display Google's sample test banners. The currently available v0.4.1 closed-testing release does not display ads. When an approved advertising-enabled release is distributed, Google AdMob may display ads for free users, while an active Lifetime Pro entitlement removes banners.

Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) can show a privacy message to request consent or offer applicable privacy choices before advertising starts. Advertising requests are made only when the SDK reports that ad requests are permitted under its consent configuration. Depending on your region and choices, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable.

To review or withdraw advertising consent in the app: Open CheckMyJobJob → Settings → Ad Privacy Options when this option is available for your device and region. This reopens Google's privacy options form. You may also manage advertising identifiers through your Android device settings. If the in-app option is not shown and you have a question, contact the developer using the email below.

Optional Lifetime Pro purchase

In versions where purchasing is enabled, CheckMyJobJob offers an optional one-time Lifetime Pro license through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing and purchase verification; the developer does not receive your payment card number. The app checks purchase ownership using Google Play to unlock paid features and ad-free use. A Pro entitlement may be restored when the purchasing Google Play account is available.

Storage, retention, and backups

Saved schedule information remains in the app's local storage until you change or delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Android backup features, when enabled, may back up app data through your device or backup provider. Temporary shared images may be retained in the app's cache until cleared by Android or app-data deletion. Google's retention of advertising or purchase data is governed by its own policies.

Security and deletion

CheckMyJobJob relies on Android's app-storage isolation and user-initiated sharing permissions. You can remove saved jobs and shifts within the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes its local data; any separately exported files or device/cloud backups may need to be deleted where they are stored.

Children

CheckMyJobJob is not designed or directed specifically for children.

Privacy inquiries

Contact the developer at broughtvulture.apps@gmail.com with privacy questions, including questions about advertising consent. You can also use the developer contact information on the Google Play listing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features, SDKs, or data practices change. The latest version will remain available at this page with an updated date.
